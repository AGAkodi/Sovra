## If you record it yourself

- **Where:** a quiet room, phone 20–30 cm from your mouth, the voice-recorder app.
- **Takes:** one take per row is fine. Leave 1 second of silence before and after each.
- **Files:** name them `vo-01.m4a` … `vo-13.m4a`, or record it all in one file, `vo-full.m4a`.
- **Drop them in:** `video/film/assets/voice/`.
- **What I do next:** HyperFrames transcribes them for word timings. I then retime each scene to your words (the voice becomes the film's clock) and set the levels.
